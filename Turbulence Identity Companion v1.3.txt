Turbulence Identity Package — Specialist Companion
Purpose
This companion is the execution and interpretation manual for the turbulence identity package. It is written for a specialist who needs to move from the scientific protocol in The Turbulence Identity Problem to the released machine contract, execute that contract against the Structural Flow Universal Kernel, and report the result without confusing formal conformance with empirical truth.
The companion does not redefine turbulence, alter the scientific protocol, or create a second scientific authority. It explains how the released scientific and machine objects fit together and what an independent executor must record at each step.
1. Package architecture
The public package contains four functional layers.
1.1 Scientific anchor
The Turbulence Identity Problem is the field-facing scientific authority for this package.
It owns:
* the turbulence identity question;
* the exact declared first-closure scope, Σ_C;
* the DLBTF and HFN scientific burdens;
* the roster and scientific roles of the test cases;
* the empirical protocol;
* the meanings of the scientific result states;
* the global dispositions CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C, NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C, and NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C;
* the scientific claim ceiling.
At the time of this companion release, the audited anchor is The Turbulence Identity Problem v1.2. An execution must record the exact compatible anchor version it actually uses.
1.2 Turbulence machine bundle
The released turbulence-specific machine package is:
Turbulence_DLBTF_HFN_Compound_Identity_APPEND_BUNDLE_v1.0.lean
It contains:
* DLBTF Identity Adapter + Contract v1.2;
* DLBTF + HFN Compound Identity Adapter v0.5.
The bundle maps already-adjudicated turbulence-domain states into formal predicates, contracts, certificates, blockers, and conditional implications.
It does not determine whether the underlying physical evidence is true or adequate.
1.3 Universal Kernel
The turbulence bundle executes over:
Structural_Flow_Universal_Kernel_v1.0.lean
The Universal Kernel supplies reusable domain-neutral contract grammar. The turbulence program does not modify that universal structure.
The Kernel does not replace the scientific anchor, the turbulence adapter, or domain scientific authority.
1.4 This companion
This companion owns public guidance for:
* package identification;

The companion may explain the other package layers. It may not outrank them in the domains they own.
2. Authority chain
The package uses a strict authority boundary:
PHYSICS DESCRIBES THE PHYSICAL REALIZATION AND THE EVIDENCE BEARING ON EACH SCIENTIFIC BURDEN.
THE FROZEN SCIENTIFIC PROTOCOL ADJUDICATES THE APPLICABLE SCIENTIFIC STATE.
THE ADAPTER MAPS THAT ADJUDICATED STATE INTO THE MACHINE CONTRACT.
LEAN CHECKS THE ENCODED CONDITIONAL STRUCTURE.
THE COMPANION EXPLAINS EXECUTION, INTERPRETATION, AND REPORTING.
Each sentence does a different job.
2.1 Physics and domain evidence
Physics remains authoritative over the physical realization and over the evidence from which the protocol must decide.
This includes, among other things:
* whether a flow or comparator has the independently established scientific standing assigned to it;
* whether a case lies within Σ_C or has the required comparator standing;
* the β observables and calibrated tolerances;
* whether β is actually preserved;
* whether a transfer or history representation is competent;
* whether an intervention is physically competent;
* whether an enlargement axis is physical and remains within the same regime family;
* statistical power, numerical convergence, and uncertainty;
* whether recurrence discrimination is adequate;
* whether a synthetic comparator satisfies or fails the required physical provenance;
* empirical truth.
Neither Lean nor the adapter manufactures any of those facts.
2.2 Frozen protocol
The scientific protocol owns the predeclared rules that convert evidence into an adjudicated scientific state.
Those rules determine, where applicable, whether a burden is:
* discharged;
* violated;
* unresolved;
or whether a domain-specific status such as BOUNDED, OPENING, UNRESOLVED, NONDEGENERATE, RECURRENT-THIN, or a provenance status has been earned.
The information order matters. A decision rule fixed after seeing the decisive result is not equivalent to one frozen before outcome inspection.
2.3 Turbulence adapter and contract
The turbulence adapter owns the machine-side representation of those adjudicated states.
It defines the turbulence-specific:
* entry and downstream burdens;
* seat roles;
* DLBTF physical faces;
* transfer-support statuses;
* HFN faces;
* history-family statuses;
* external-mimic provenance statuses;
* composite pass and exclusion predicates;
* adverse, blocking, open, invalid, redundancy, and residual-identity certificates;
* compound IdentitySupported predicate.
The adapter maps. It does not independently adjudicate the physics.
2.4 Universal Kernel
The Universal Kernel supplies the reusable formal contract surface beneath the turbulence adapter.
Among other things, it supplies the common three-way Disposition vocabulary and the universal entry/downstream contract grammar used by the turbulence package.
A domain-specific scientific meaning is not created merely by the generic word violated, open, or discharged. The turbulence adapter and scientific protocol determine what a state means at a particular burden location.
2.5 Lean
Lean checks the formal objects that have been supplied to it.
A successful execution may establish that:
* the populated states satisfy the encoded definitions;
* a named composite predicate follows from those states;
* a formal certificate or blocker is constructible;
* the released contract composes as declared.
Lean does not establish:
* that a real flow is turbulent or nonturbulent;
* that empirical Σ_C membership is correct;
* that β was physically preserved;
* that CTRR was measured correctly;
* that an intervention was competent;
* that BOUNDED or OPENING is physically true;
* that NONDEGENERATE or RECURRENT-THIN is physically true;
* that physical provenance is satisfied or failed;
* that the experiment was actually performed under the frozen protocol;
* that turbulence identity is empirically closed.
Machine-clean is not physics-confirmed.

4. Anchor compatibility
The turbulence machine bundle is intentionally not tied forever to one anchor version number.
A later released version of The Turbulence Identity Problem may remain compatible with the same machine bundle if the later anchor preserves the machine-relevant semantics that the bundle encodes.
Compatibility requires preservation of the relevant:
* DLBTF ∧ HFN identity criterion;
* scientific burden meanings;
* status spaces;
* role-routing logic;
* scientific-to-machine mapping;
* scope relation represented at the machine boundary;
* global result-routing logic that bears on the machine interface.
Changes limited to prose, exposition, references, organization, reporting language, or other non-machine-semantic material do not by themselves require a new adapter or contract.
A later anchor is not automatically compatible merely because it carries the same title. If an anchor changes a machine-relevant scientific burden, scope relation, role meaning, status meaning, adjudication route, or encoded dependency, compatibility must be re-adjudicated before that anchor is used with the existing machine bundle.
This rule prevents two opposite errors:
* version chasing, in which harmless scientific-document iteration needlessly forces machine releases;
* silent semantic drift, in which a changed scientific contract is used with an adapter that still encodes an older one.
5. Machine-package version discipline
The bundle and its contained components also version independently.
A packaging, header, or documentation-only change may change the outer bundle version without requiring a contained component version change.
A semantic change to the DLBTF adapter / contract should change the DLBTF component version and the containing bundle version.
A semantic change to the compound adapter should change the compound component version and the containing bundle version.
A Universal Kernel change is independent and is warranted only when the pressure reaches reusable domain-neutral machine structure.
The public execution record should therefore never infer compatibility from matching version numbers. Compatibility is a semantic relation, not a numbering coincidence.

8. The closure firewall
The strongest supportive machine predicate in the released compound adapter is IdentitySupported.
Its meaning is formal:
the supplied machine states satisfy the released encoded compound-support contract.
Its non-meaning is equally important:
Lean has not thereby proved that actual turbulence is DLBTF ∧ HFN.
Scientific closure requires two things together:
SCIENTIFICALLY VALID, FAITHFULLY POPULATED EXECUTION RECORD
+
MACHINE IdentitySupported
→
SCIENTIFIC CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C
The final scientific disposition remains anchor-owned.
Likewise, an adverse or open machine certificate receives scientific meaning only after the scientific record supporting the populated states has been checked. A machine proposition cannot turn an invalid or unfaithfully populated execution into empirical evidence.

When scientific and machine language appear together, keep the direction of authority explicit:
* evidence comes from physics;

* scientific reporting returns to the anchor-owned disposition and claim ceiling.
The purpose of the machine layer is auditability of the conditional structure, not replacement of scientific judgment.
10. Scientific object
Within the declared first-closure scope Σ_C, the candidate empirical identity is:
turbulence ⇔ DLBTF ∧ HFN
where:
DLBTF = Distributed Load-Bearing Transfer Flow
and:
HFN = History-Family Nondegeneracy.
The equivalence is the hypothesis being tested. It is not a result supplied in advance.
DLBTF and HFN remain separate because they answer different physical failure questions.
DLBTF asks whether the nonlinear-transfer capacity required by the regime remains dependent on causally load-bearing transfer support that does not close to one uniformly bounded physical transfer interface across the declared physical enlargement ladder.
HFN asks whether the independently established regime supports a nondegenerate family of realized histories, or instead collapses, after the frozen quotient and recurrence test, onto a dynamically thin recurrent itinerary or finite thin recurrent family.
The two burdens are conjunctive but not interchangeable.
A flow may pressure or fail one side while surviving the other. Combining them into one generic “turbulence score” would erase the location of a scientifically meaningful failure.
For each flow, the two scientific assays therefore retain their own three-way status spaces:
DLBTF
* BOUNDED
* OPENING
* UNRESOLVED
HFN
* NONDEGENERATE
* RECURRENT-THIN
* UNRESOLVED
Failure to establish OPENING does not establish BOUNDED.
Failure to establish NONDEGENERATE does not establish RECURRENT-THIN.
An unresolved result remains unresolved unless some separate competent burden already supplies a decisive falsifier.
For a required turbulent positive or validly opened holdout:
* competent BOUNDED defeats DLBTF necessity;
* competent RECURRENT-THIN defeats HFN necessity;
* one demonstrated necessary-conjunct failure is sufficient to defeat the proposed conjunction at that seat.
For a nonturbulent comparator:
* competent DLBTF failure may exclude it;
* competent HFN failure may exclude it;
* where the comparator is a synthetic false substitute, competent failure of the required physical provenance may also exclude it;
* competent satisfaction of the complete applicable criterion pressures sufficiency.
The result at one flow is therefore not simply “turbulent” or “not turbulent.” It is a located record of which independently specified identity burdens passed, failed, or remained unresolved under the frozen protocol.
11. Declared first-closure scope
The first experiment is deliberately narrower than “all turbulence.”
The exact anchor-owned first-closure scope is:
Σ_C = established three-dimensional, single-phase, constant-property, non-rotating, non-stratified, non-buoyant, incompressible Newtonian Navier–Stokes turbulence in canonical homogeneous, free-shear and smooth-wall flow families, away from laminar–turbulent transition.
That declaration is part of the scientific contract.
It must not be widened by shorthand in the companion, by a broad use of the word “turbulence,” or by a machine execution that happens to accept a seat representation formally.
The following are outside the first-closure jurisdiction:
* compressible turbulence;
* rotating turbulence;
* strongly stratified or buoyant turbulence;
* rough-wall turbulence;
* strongly curved or separated-wall regimes;
* multiphase or reactive turbulence;
* magnetohydrodynamic or plasma turbulence;
* quantum or superfluid turbulence;
* generic spatiotemporal chaos.
These are not negative cases for the first experiment.
They are outside the declared jurisdiction.
A successful result inside Σ_C therefore does not silently extend to them.
Likewise, a case outside Σ_C cannot be inserted as an adversarial negative merely because it would be convenient for the discrimination test. The positive and negative surfaces must remain scientifically admissible for the role assigned to them.
11.1 Scope at the machine boundary
The turbulence contract carries explicit machine burdens for scope binding.

On the HFN / compound side:

The Universal Kernel also carries its domain-neutral scope pin as part of the universal translation surface.
These machine burdens do not determine whether a physical flow actually belongs in Σ_C.
That remains a scientific judgment supported by the domain record.
The machine checks that the declared scope relationship has been populated consistently with the contract. It does not independently classify the physics.
For confirmatory execution, the record should therefore contain both:
1. the exact Σ_C text from the compatible anchor;
2. the scientific basis for admitting each relevant positive flow or comparator to the role assigned to it.
A scope question left genuinely unresolved remains a blocker.
A post-result scope change used to remove an inconvenient result is execution-invalid.
A case that is outside jurisdiction remains outside jurisdiction rather than becoming a negative result.
12. Frozen scientific roster
The roster is a validation and adjudication surface.
It is not the definition of turbulence.
The scientific standing of a seat is established independently of DLBTF, HFN, and the desired experimental outcome. That independence is what allows the test to fail honestly.
12.1 Core positive families
The frozen core-positive surface is:
P1 — forced homogeneous isotropic turbulence
P2 — freely decaying homogeneous isotropic turbulence
P3 — homogeneous shear turbulence
P4 — primary internal smooth-wall turbulent channel
P5 — turbulent round jet
P6 — zero-pressure-gradient turbulent boundary layer
These six families expose the candidate to substantial variation within Σ_C.
They are not declared turbulent because they satisfy DLBTF ∧ HFN.
Their scientific standing precedes the proposed criterion.
Accordingly, if one validly executed core positive returns a competent necessary-conjunct failure, the candidate is answerable to that result. The flow is not removed from the positive surface after the fact.
12.2 Negative and adversarial comparators
The frozen adversarial surface is:
N1 — laminar periodic cylinder wake
N2 — steady or traveling exact coherent solution
N3 — periodic or quasiperiodic coherent branch
N4 — low-dimensional chaotic Taylor–Couette case
N5 — synthetic or fake-turbulence false substitute
N6 — laminar zero-pressure-gradient flat-plate boundary layer
These cases do not form one physical alternative class.
Their common job is adversarial.
They preserve different pieces of what an overly permissive turbulence criterion might mistake for identity:
* coherent fluid dynamics;
* nonlinear transfer;
* recurrence;
* chaos;
* turbulence-like statistics;
* related geometry or flow organization.
Different comparators are therefore allowed to be excluded for different reasons.
A negative case does not have to fail both DLBTF and HFN.
One competent necessary-burden failure is enough.
For N5, competent failure of the required physical provenance supplies an additional scientifically permitted exclusion route.
The dangerous result is the opposite one: a scientifically admissible nonturbulent comparator that survives the complete applicable identity burden.
That is a sufficiency pressure, not a case to be reclassified after the result.
12.3 Recurrent boundary pair
The frozen boundary pair is:
B1a — isolated recurrent / unstable-periodic-orbit object
B1b — containing turbulent regime family
These two seats test whether the criterion respects physical level.
An isolated recurrent object and the larger regime that approaches, shadows, leaves, and revisits recurrent structures are not the same scientific object.
Their identities and levels are fixed before decisive adjudication.
The protocol does not require a preselected result pair.
For example, B1a may legitimately return RECURRENT-THIN while B1b returns NONDEGENERATE, but that pattern is an allowed scientific result rather than a machine requirement.
The hard requirement is level coherence.
The execution may not inspect the result and then switch between “orbit” and “regime” descriptions to protect the criterion.
12.4 Protected within-family holdouts
The protected portability tests are:
V1 — turbulent pipe or plane Couette
V2 — turbulent wake or mixing layer
The exact implementation selected for each holdout belongs to the frozen scientific protocol.
The holdouts are kept separate from criterion development so that they can test whether the frozen method carries beyond its primary internal-wall and free-shear implementations.
Once a holdout is validly opened for decisive adjudication, it carries the same necessity burden as a core positive.
It is not an optional confirmation.
A validly opened holdout returning competent BOUNDED or RECURRENT-THIN defeats the corresponding necessity claim.
A required holdout that remains unresolved prevents class-level closure unless some independent valid result has already established NOT CLOSED.
If opening a holdout reveals that the frozen method itself is unusable, that execution is invalid for confirmatory use. The exposed holdout result cannot then be used to retune the method and adjudicate the same holdout again as though it had remained protected.

14. Scope-and-role execution rule
Before any β, DLBTF, HFN, provenance, or compound result is allowed to carry identity weight, the confirmatory record should make the following answerable for every seat:
* What physical case is this?
* What is its independent scientific standing?
* Is it inside Σ_C, or what comparator jurisdiction licenses its use?

The next layer is β: the common fidelity burden that determines whether the compared states, interventions, continuations, and histories still answer for the same independently established regime.
15. β — the shared fidelity burden
Every decisive flow is evaluated under a preregistered reference-regime fidelity specification, denoted β.
β answers a basic question:
Does the comparison still answer for the same independently established physical regime in the ways that matter to this assay?
The concrete observables may differ from one flow family to another. A round jet, homogeneous isotropic turbulence, channel flow, and a boundary layer do not have identical natural diagnostics. What remains invariant is the scientific job β must perform.
For every decisive flow, β carries four requirements:
1. preservation of the relevant budgets;
2. preservation of continuation or development of the reference regime;
3. preservation of the causal-response behavior used by the assay;
4. preservation of the state-distribution features required to establish that the comparison still represents the same independently established regime.
The criterion therefore does not reduce fidelity to one spectrum, one profile, one statistic, or one visual resemblance.
A reduced interface that reproduces familiar statistics while corrupting a relevant budget has not preserved β.
A system that preserves the budget but destroys the required temporal or downstream continuation has not preserved β.
A system that retains both but changes the intervention-response behavior on which the causal assay depends has not preserved β.
The same rule applies in the other direction: matching the causal response while failing the required state-distribution or regime-standing burden does not establish β either.
All four jobs remain live.
15.1 One fidelity question, flow-specific observables
The protocol permits flow-specific observables because the physics differs across the roster. It does not permit different epistemic standards. For each seat, the specialist must instantiate the four β jobs using observables appropriate to that physical case and freeze the associated mappings and tolerances before decisive adjudication. The resulting β implementations may therefore look different while performing the same scientific work. This distinction is important:
same scientific job does not require identical measurement coordinates.
15.2 Positive and negative symmetry
β applies to both sides of the discrimination problem. A negative comparator cannot be excluded merely because it was subjected to a stricter fidelity rule than the positives. A positive cannot be protected by a weaker rule. Where the scientific role permits comparison, the same four fidelity jobs remain answerable.
Consequently:
   * a positive flow that competently fails a necessary conjunct after satisfying its preregistered β burden presents a genuine necessity problem;
   * a nonturbulent comparator that satisfies the complete applicable criterion under its preregistered β burden presents a genuine sufficiency problem.
β is therefore part of the firewall against an asymmetric test.
15.3 Freeze and qualification are different burdens
The machine contract distinguishes between fixing the β rule and establishing that β was actually satisfied. Before decisive outcomes, the DLBTF entry surface includes:
betaDefinitionFrozen (seat)
and:
betaDecisionRuleFrozen (seat).
These record that the scientific meaning of β and the rule used to adjudicate it were fixed before the outcome. They do not establish that a particular seat later preserved β. Actual per-seat adjudication enters downstream through:
betaQualification (seat).
The released predicate:
BetaQualifiedAtSeat
requires that downstream β qualification to be discharged.
The distinction is:
β definition frozen ≠ β satisfied.
β decision rule frozen ≠ β satisfied.
The former protects information order. The latter is a scientific result.
15.4 One β verdict shared by DLBTF and HFN
The compound package does not create a second independent β verdict for HFN.
The history-side evaluation reuses the same per-seat BetaQualifiedAtSeat carried by the transfer contract. This matters because DLBTF and HFN are supposed to answer different identity questions about the same independently established regime. They may interrogate different structures, but they do not get to preserve different versions of the flow. The execution record should therefore contain one authoritative β qualification per seat, supported by the complete four-job scientific record.
15.5 β failure routing
A β problem does not automatically answer the turbulence identity question.
If the per-seat β evidence is inadequate, contradictory, or unresolved, the relevant identity assay loses authority to use that result.
At the machine level:
      * betaQualification = discharged permits the β-dependent evaluation to proceed;
      * betaQualification = violated is an adjudication blocker;
      * betaQualification = open is an unresolved adjudication blocker.
A failed β qualification is not automatically:
      * BOUNDED;
      * OPENING;
      * RECURRENT-THIN;
      * NONDEGENERATE;
      * a necessity failure;
      * a sufficiency failure.
It says the comparison did not preserve the required fidelity burden strongly enough to carry the intended identity inference. Absent another independently earned adverse result, the scientific route remains NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C.
A different problem occurs if the β definition or decision rule itself was changed after decisive outcomes. That is not merely an inadequate assay result. It is an execution-integrity failure.
16. DLBTF — the transfer-side question
Distributed Load-Bearing Transfer Flow asks whether the nonlinear-transfer capacity required by the independently established regime can be replaced by one uniformly bounded physical transfer interface while β remains satisfied across admissible physical enlargement.
The operative question is not:
How large is the numerical representation?
It is:
How much causally sufficient physical transfer support must remain available for the β-relevant regime burden to continue carrying as the physical problem is enlarged?
That distinction excludes several common substitutions. DLBTF is not raw:
         * grid size;
         * Fourier-mode count;
         * latent dimension;
         * state-space dimension;
         * attractor dimension;
         * number of nonlinear terms.
Those quantities may matter to an implementation, but none is the DLBTF object merely by being large.
The object is the physical transfer interface through which β-relevant causal continuation is carried.
16.1 Physical enlargement
The enlargement axis must enlarge the physical problem rather than merely the numerical description.
Increasing numerical resolution, adding basis functions, reducing the time step, or increasing floating-point precision does not by itself constitute physical enlargement.
An admissible enlargement changes a preregistered physical control while keeping the case within the same independently established regime family and declared scope.
The machine entry surface therefore separates:
lawfulEnlargementAxisPinned (seat)
from:
enlargementLadderFrozen (seat).
The first records which physical direction of enlargement has been declared. The second records the finite ladder over which the confirmatory assay will be executed.
Whether that physical axis is scientifically appropriate remains a domain judgment.
16.2 The bounded-interface challenge
The BOUNDED side is existential.
One competent bounded physical transfer-interface construction is enough.
The challenger is allowed to use a sophisticated interface. Memory terms, nonlocal couplings, auxiliary variables, stochastic internal states, latent variables, learned dynamics, or modified evolution operators may all be part of a proposed bounded construction if they actually carry information across the relevant causal cut.
They must, however, be counted.
A nominally small interface cannot earn BOUNDED by hiding required transfer capacity in uncounted memory, state, coupling, or opaque latent machinery.
For that reason, the entry surface separately freezes:
reductionClosureFamilyFrozen (seat)
and:
closureAccountingRuleFrozen (seat).
BOUNDED is earned when a competent bounded transfer-interface schema survives the preregistered physical enlargement ladder while preserving the full β burden under the frozen accounting rule.
No exhaustive search over every imaginable model is required.
One successful competent construction is sufficient.
16.3 Failed searches do not establish OPENING
The converse inference is prohibited.
A failed bounded-interface search establishes only that the tested candidate failed. Many failed searches still establish only that many tested candidates failed.
They do not establish that no competent bounded interface exists.
The machine therefore carries an explicit:
supportSearchAdequacy (seat)
burden, but adequate bounded-interface search is not itself the positive evidence for OPENING.
OPENING requires a different evidentiary route: a positive lower bound on causally required transfer capacity.
That is the job of CTRR.
17. Transfer-support status
For each applicable seat, the scientific transfer-support result is exactly one of:
         * BOUNDED
         * OPENING
         * UNRESOLVED
The machine field is:
supportStatus.
Its constructors are:
         * bounded
         * opening
         * unresolved
These states are mutually distinct scientific claims.
17.1 BOUNDED
BOUNDED means that a competent uniformly bounded physical transfer-interface schema preserves β across the preregistered physical enlargement ladder under the frozen closure-accounting rule.
At a required turbulent positive or validly opened holdout, competent BOUNDED is a direct DLBTF necessity falsifier.
At an appropriate negative comparator, BOUNDED can instead supply a valid exclusion route.
The same status therefore has different global significance depending on the independently frozen scientific role of the seat.
17.2 OPENING
OPENING is a positive finite experimental claim.
It requires evidence that the lower bound on causally required transfer capacity continues to grow beyond uncertainty across the powered physical enlargement ladder while the required adequacy, novelty, and load-bearing controls remain satisfied.
OPENING does not mean:
         * infinite complexity has been proved;
         * CTRR diverges under indefinite enlargement;
         * no finite turbulence model can exist;
         * the tested rank is a global minimum model dimension;
         * every untested realization has the same property.
The result is bounded by the physical ladder actually tested.
17.3 UNRESOLVED
UNRESOLVED means that neither BOUNDED nor OPENING has been competently earned.
A powered plateau may block OPENING without establishing BOUNDED.
An inadequate bounded-interface search may block a particular inference without establishing OPENING.
A representation disagreement may leave the transfer question unresolved. Insufficient power may do the same.
UNRESOLVED is therefore not a compromise value between BOUNDED and OPENING.
It is the statement that neither positive scientific claim has yet earned the required evidentiary standing.
18. The four DLBTF constitutive faces
Transfer-support status does not carry identity weight by itself.
The released adapter also requires four physical faces of the DLBTF claim:
         * realizedHistory
         * nonlinearTransfer
         * loadBearingness
         * causalContinuation
These are represented through the per-seat machine face state and assembled by:
PhysicalFacesPass.
A face-level violation is exposed through the constitutive-face violation route.
18.1 Realized-history standing
The transfer claim must concern realized physical continuation of the relevant case rather than a merely formal or static possibility.
The scientific record must establish that the assay is interrogating the realized-history burden the candidate identity is supposed to describe.
18.2 Nonlinear-transfer involvement
The relevant continuation must actually involve the nonlinear-transfer structure being interrogated.
A response that is entirely carried by some unrelated mechanism does not become evidence for DLBTF merely because nonlinear terms exist elsewhere in the model.
18.3 Load-bearingness
The identified transfer support must matter to the β-relevant function.
A mathematically distinguishable response direction is not identity-bearing merely because it increases a matrix rank.
The positive assay therefore has to show that removing or suppressing the claimed support produces the required β-relevant deficit under the frozen intervention design.
18.4 Causal continuation
The claimed transfer directions must bear on distinguishable β-relevant future behavior.
The question is not merely whether an intervention produces some disturbance.
It is whether independent changes to nonlinear transfer produce independently distinguishable future consequences that matter to the frozen β burden.
18.5 Face violations and scientific meaning
A face state uses the generic machine vocabulary:
         * discharged
         * violated
         * open
The released predicate ConstitutiveFaceViolated locates at least one explicitly violated DLBTF face at a seat.
Its scientific meaning still depends on adequacy and role: a competent constitutive-face violation can function as a local identity falsifier only when the surrounding EvaluationAdequate burden is independently satisfied.
The word violated alone is never enough to determine the global scientific result.
19. DLBTF evaluation adequacy
Before a transfer-support status can carry identity-level weight, the released machine contract requires a base adequacy predicate:

         * supportSearchAdequacy

         * BetaQualifiedAtSeat
Each performs a different scientific job.
19.1 Support adjudication
supportAdjudication records that the transfer-support verdict itself was competently adjudicated under the frozen scientific rule.
It is not the same thing as the supportStatus value.
The status says what the result is.
The adjudication burden says whether that result is competent for identity use.
19.2 Representation neutrality
No one transfer representation is allowed to dictate the identity result merely by coordinate choice.
The protocol requires competent alternative representations that expose the transfer burden differently.
Their raw coordinates and raw ranks need not be identical. For identity-level use, however, they must support the same physical transfer-status conclusion directly or through a preregistered mapping that preserves the relevant physical comparison.
If competent representations cannot be reconciled under the frozen rule, the transfer status remains unresolved rather than being averaged into a preferred answer.
19.3 Search adequacy
The bounded-interface challenger search must satisfy its preregistered coverage criterion.
This requirement keeps the BOUNDED/OPENING comparison scientifically serious.
It still does not turn failed searches into positive OPENING evidence.
19.4 Power adequacy
The transfer result must clear the frozen uncertainty, numerical, statistical, and intervention-related evidentiary standard.
A rank pattern below the required power or uncertainty threshold cannot be promoted into a transfer verdict merely because its qualitative trend is suggestive.
19.5 Enlargement robustness
The result must survive the declared physical enlargement ladder while retaining the same independently established regime family and β burden.
An apparent opening that disappears once the physical parameter is enlarged competently has not survived the intended test.
An enlargement that leaves the declared family or crosses the excluded transition boundary is not a valid continuation of the same seat.
19.6 Base adequacy is role-neutral
EvaluationAdequate does not itself say whether the transfer result is good or bad for the identity hypothesis.
It says the support-status result is competent enough to be used.
The role of the seat and the status then determine the scientific consequence.

         * suppressionAdequacy
         * matchedShamAdequacy
         * restorationOrReplacementAdequacy

20.1 Causal novelty
Growth in response rank is not enough if the larger system merely repeats, rescales, or inherits copies of the same bounded response structure.
The smaller-system response subspace is therefore mapped into the larger one under a preregistered correspondence.
The larger system must contain β-relevant causal response content outside the inherited, rescaled, or replicated smaller-system subspace.
That is the scientific job of:
causalNoveltyAdequacy.
20.2 Suppression
The rank-bearing directions identified by the positive assay must carry load.
Under the frozen suppression design, suppressing those directions must produce the required β-relevant deficit.
That burden is represented by:
suppressionAdequacy.
20.3 Matched sham
An intervention can disturb a flow without removing the function being claimed.
The suppression deficit must therefore be established relative to the preregistered matched-sham comparison rather than attributed automatically to intervention disturbance.
That burden is:
matchedShamAdequacy.
20.4 Restoration or competent replacement
If the claimed transfer support is genuinely carrying the β-relevant function, restoring the suppressed capacity—or supplying a competence-matched replacement under the frozen rule—must recover the missing function to the required degree.
That burden is:
restorationOrReplacementAdequacy.
20.5 Failure of an OPENING control
Failure to discharge one of these controls blocks the OPENING identity route.
It does not automatically establish BOUNDED.
It does not automatically falsify DLBTF.
It does not automatically return NOT CLOSED.
Absent another independently earned adverse result, the scientific question remains unresolved at the affected burden.
The associated designs and decision rules are frozen before outcome use under the transfer-side pre-outcome integrity burden.
21. CTRR — the positive lower-bound bridge
Causal Transfer Response Rank, CTRR, supplies the positive lower-bound route needed for OPENING.
Its purpose is narrow:
lower-bound the number of independent transfer-interface directions required to carry distinguishable β-relevant causal response under the declared local factorization assumptions.
It is not a count of all turbulent degrees of freedom.
21.1 Local response operator
For one independently established flow (X), physical enlargement level λ, reference state (x), and competent transfer representation (R), let:
a ∈ ℝᵖ
denote a normalized intervention applied to independently defined directions of the nonlinear-transfer contribution.
Let:
y ∈ ℝᑫ
denote the preregistered normalized future response entering the β fidelity burden.
The intervention defines a local response map:
Fᴿ(X, λ, x; a) = y,
with the unperturbed reference at a = 0.
Its first-order causal response operator is:
Jᴿ(X, λ, x) = DₐFᴿ(X, λ, x; 0).
The dimensions (p) and (q) describe the chosen intervention and response coordinates.
They are not themselves measures of turbulence complexity.
The object of interest is the number of independent intervention directions that remain distinguishable in β-relevant future response.
21.2 Factorization lower bound
Suppose a proposed β-sufficient physical realization carries the intervention effect through (m) independent transfer-interface coordinates:
z ∈ ℝᵐ
with factorization:
a → z(a) → y(z).
Where the declared local maps are differentiable at the reference state:
Dₐy = D_z y · Dₐz.
Therefore:
rank(Dₐy) ≤ m.
Hence:
m ≥ rank(Jᴿ(X, λ, x)).
The conclusion is conditional and local.
Under the stated factorization and regularity assumptions, the measured causal response rank supplies a lower bound on the number of independent transfer-interface coordinates through which a β-sufficient response can pass.
It is not a theorem that every nonlinear turbulence model globally factorizes in this way.
21.3 Tolerance-aware CTRR
Exact matrix rank is too brittle for empirical use.
Let εβ denote the frozen total operator-level error allowance after the permitted causal-response discrepancy and the relevant estimation, numerical, and intervention-linearization uncertainties are included.
Let:
σ₁ ≥ σ₂ ≥ ··· ≥ σᵣ ≥ 0
be the singular values of the normalized response operator.
For the spectral norm, the optimal error of any rank-m approximation is σₘ₊₁.
Therefore, if:
σₘ₊₁ > εβ,
then no rank-m linear response operator can approximate the measured normalized response operator within the frozen tolerance.
Define the β-tolerant Causal Transfer Response Rank:
CTRRεβ(X, λ, x, R) = #{i : σᵢ > εβ}.
Combining this approximation bound with the differentiable-factorization result from Section 21.2, any β-sufficient physical transfer realization within the declared regularity class must carry at least CTRRεβ independent causal transfer-interface dimensions at that state.


This is the positive lower-bound step.


Because the tolerance-aware count depends on normalization, the scaling of intervention and response coordinates is part of preregistration. Equivalent-basis normalization checks are required so an apparent change in CTRR cannot be manufactured by coordinate rescaling while leaving the underlying response geometry unchanged.


The inference remains local to the declared intervention family, observation horizon, factorization assumptions, and regularity class.


It does not identify:
         * the dimension of the complete Navier–Stokes state;
         * an attractor dimension;
         * an inertial-manifold dimension;
         * a universal minimal turbulence-model dimension.
A proposed bounded interface also cannot evade the lower bound merely by hiding independent causal-response routes inside memory variables, stochastic internal states, learned coordinates, delayed variables, auxiliary equations, or other uncounted machinery.
Where such structures carry independent causal response across the declared transfer interface, they belong on the transfer side of the causal cut and must be accounted for under the frozen closure rule.
21.4 From state-level response to regime-level evidence
A single state is not the regime. At each physical enlargement level λ and competent representation R, the experiment samples the preregistered ensemble of β-qualified reference states and estimates CTRR with uncertainty for each state.
Define the regime-level lower bound:
Lβᴿ(X, λ)
as the largest integer ℓ for which the preregistered required fraction of β-qualified states has a lower confidence bound of at least ℓ. The required fraction and confidence rule are frozen during calibration. This prevents:
         * one exceptionally high-rank state from establishing OPENING;
         * a convenient averaging rule from making systematically high-rank states disappear.
The resulting regime-level transfer claim must still satisfy:
         * β qualification;
         * representation neutrality;
         * power;
         * physical-enlargement robustness;
         * bounded-search adequacy;
         * causal novelty;
         * suppression;
         * matched sham;
         * restoration or competent replacement.
CTRR supplies the state-level positive lower-bound bridge. Lβᴿ carries that lower bound into the preregistered regime-level enlargement test. Neither quantity is the entire OPENING verdict by itself.
21.5 Representation discipline
Equivalent competent representations need not return identical raw singular values or identical coordinate-level ranks. What they must support is the same scientifically relevant transfer-support conclusion under the frozen cross-representation rule. A representation-specific rank that disappears or reverses under another independently competent representation cannot simply be selected because it favors the desired outcome. Unresolved competent disagreement remains unresolved.
21.6 Finite-ladder ceiling
An OPENING result remains a finite experimental statement. OPENING requires supported growth of Lβᴿ(X, λ) across the preregistered physical enlargement ladder beyond its uncertainty envelope while the required adequacy, novelty, and load-bearing controls survive. A statistically powered plateau blocks OPENING. It does not by itself establish BOUNDED. Growth produced only by numerical refinement, without physical enlargement, does not count. Growth produced solely by duplicating an already bounded causal module does not count. A competent finite ladder may therefore support:
Lβᴿ(X, λ₁) < Lβᴿ(X, λ₂) < ··· 
over the tested enlargement range.
It does not establish:
lim λ→∞ Lβᴿ(X, λ) = ∞.
Nor does it establish that no finite predictive or useful model can exist. The claim is about the tested causal transfer burden, under the tested physical enlargement, at the tested scientific scope.

23. HFN — the history-side question
History-Family Nondegeneracy asks a different question from DLBTF.
DLBTF asks what causally sufficient transfer support is required for the β-relevant regime burden.
HFN asks whether the same β-qualified regime supports a genuinely nondegenerate family of realized histories, or whether its admissible histories collapse onto a dynamically thin recurrent family at the frozen scientific level.
The operative question is:
Does the regime itself sustain distinct realized continuations that remain within the same β-qualified regime and escape exhaustion by the preregistered thin recurrent class?
HFN is not established by dynamical complexity in the abstract.
In particular, none of the following is sufficient by itself:
            * a positive Lyapunov exponent;
            * sensitive dependence;
            * aperiodicity;
            * many initial conditions;
            * many forcing realizations;
            * stochastic forcing;
            * a large state space;
            * a large number of observed trajectories.
Those facts may contribute evidence.
They do not perform the whole HFN job.
23.1 The history-side freeze

            * compoundPreOutcomeIntegrity
These fields protect information order.
They do not themselves establish HFN.
The scientific execution must still produce the evidence that allows the frozen rules to return a history-family status.
23.2 Reference-state and perturbation discipline
Each decisive case begins from a preregistered neighborhood of β-qualified reference states.
The perturbation neighborhood is also frozen.
The purpose is not simply to generate many trajectories.
The purpose is to ask whether nearby admissible realizations can separate into genuinely distinct same-regime continuations under the regime’s own dynamics.
A perturbation that moves the system into another physical regime does not establish HFN for the original one.
A collection of trajectories that differ only because they were assigned different external labels does not establish HFN either.
23.3 Quotient before plurality
Raw coordinate difference is not enough to establish distinct history.
Two trajectories related only by an equivalence that the experiment has declared irrelevant—such as an allowed spatial symmetry, phase displacement, or time shift—must not be counted as separate histories merely because their coordinates differ.
The frozen:
symmetryPhaseTimeQuotientFrozen (seat)
burden therefore precedes the decisive history result.
HFN plurality is assessed after the declared quotient has done its work.
23.4 Recurrent-template discipline
The thin recurrent comparison class is also fixed before decisive histories are interpreted.
The purpose is not to enumerate every recurrent solution that might exist.
That would make HFN depend on proving the absence of an undiscovered orbit.
Instead, the experiment freezes a scientifically competent recurrent-template class and a recurrence rule sufficient to discriminate the claimed history-family burden at the declared scope and horizon.
Adequate discrimination is required.
Exhaustive enumeration is not.
24. The five HFN constitutive faces
The released compound adapter represents five separate HFN faces:
            * realizedHistoryFamily
            * sameRegimePlurality
            * endogenousDynamicalSeparation
            * recurrenceNonExhaustion
            * regimeLevelContinuation
They are separately adjudicated because each performs a distinct scientific job.
24.1 Realized-history family
HFN requires a family of realized histories rather than one selected trajectory.
The scientific record must establish that the assay actually contains multiple realized continuations answerable to the frozen history-family definition.
One formal orbit, one reference trajectory, or one hypothetical continuation is not a history family.
24.2 Same-regime plurality
The multiple histories must remain members of the same independently established β-qualified regime.
Plurality created by comparing different regimes does not establish HFN for either one.
The requirement is therefore stronger than:
there exist different trajectories.
It is:
there exist distinct admissible histories of the same regime.
24.3 Endogenous dynamical separation
The relevant separation must arise through the regime’s own registered dynamics.
Variation attributable only to:
            * a different forcing label;
            * a random-seed label;
            * a bookkeeping label;
            * a coordinate transformation;
            * another externally imposed distinction
does not automatically establish endogenous history-family nondegeneracy.
Random forcing may be part of the physical system.
It may not answer the endogenous-variation burden merely by generating different paths.
24.4 Recurrence non-exhaustion
The realized same-regime histories must not be exhausted by the frozen thin recurrent-template class after the declared quotient.
This is stronger than showing local trajectory separation.
An unstable periodic orbit may have expanding directions while still remaining one recurrent object.
A positive Lyapunov exponent therefore does not by itself establish recurrence non-exhaustion.
The history-family evidence must show that the admissible realized continuations cannot all be absorbed into the frozen thin recurrent class under the preregistered recurrence rule.
24.5 Regime-level continuation
The distinction between histories must persist long enough to remain a fact about regime continuation rather than a short-lived perturbation that immediately collapses back onto one thin itinerary.
The observation horizon is therefore part of the frozen scientific design.
A transient separation is not automatically a regime-level history-family result.

            * discharged
            * violated
            * open
HistoryFacesPass requires the five HFN faces to be discharged.

If an external mimic otherwise passes the compound criterion but:
            * provenance status remains unresolved;
            * provenance adjudication remains open;
            * provenance adjudication is violated;
the released machine package can expose:
ExternalMimicProvenanceOpen.
That state is a blocker.
It is neither:
            * a valid exclusion;
            * nor a sufficiency counterexample.
Absent another earned adverse result, the scientific disposition remains open.
29. Compound adjudication at one seat
DLBTF and HFN are adjudicated separately first.
The compound layer combines them only after their own scientific states and adequacy burdens have been established.
29.1 Complete positive compound pass

CompoundPassAtSeat
requires:
            * DLBTFPassAtSeat;
            * AntiReplicationAdequate;
            * HFNPassAtSeat.
The conjunction is strict.
A seat does not pass the complete candidate because one side is especially strong.
DLBTF cannot substitute for HFN.
HFN cannot substitute for DLBTF.
Anti-replication cannot be omitted merely because the two primary status labels are favorable.
29.2 Hard-negative exclusion
For a seat whose theorem-facing role is:
SeatRole.hardNegative
the released predicate:
CompoundHardNegativeExcluded
requires either:
            * TransferExcludedAtSeat;
            * HFNExcludedAtSeat.
The negative does not have to fail both conjuncts.

One established necessary-conjunct failure is sufficient to exclude the comparator.
29.3 External-mimic exclusion

SeatRole.externalMimic
the wider:

predicate applies.

            * transfer-side exclusion;
            * history-side exclusion;
            * provenance exclusion.

29.4 Boundary coherence at the machine surface

BoundaryLevelCoherent.

It does not require in advance that:
            * B1a be RECURRENT-THIN;
            * B1b be NONDEGENERATE.
It requires that each scientific object retain:
            * its own identity;
            * its own frozen level;
            * its own level-specific adjudication;
            * no post-result substitution between isolated-object and containing-regime descriptions.
The machine role boundarySeat therefore carries a coherence burden, not a preferred answer pair.
29.5 Positive roles and holdouts
Core positives and validly opened holdouts both enter the final theorem-facing positive route as:
SeatRole.positiveCore.
For either one, the machine requires:
CompoundPassAtSeat.
The scientific record remains responsible for preserving the distinction between:
            * development positive;
            * protected holdout opened for decisive adjudication.
The theorem-facing burden is the same.
The information-order history is not.
29.6 One-seat result compression
For one decisive seat, the complete compound workflow is therefore:
scientific identity and role fixed
→ β qualified
→ DLBTF adjudicated
→ HFN adjudicated
→ anti-replication adjudicated where OPENING is used

30. VIOLATED is burden-specific
The generic machine state:
Disposition.violated
does not have one universal scientific meaning.
Its consequence depends on the burden at which it appears.
The released turbulence package therefore assigns violations to five routing classes:
            * localIdentityFalsifier
            * adjudicationBlocker
            * executionInvalid
            * redundancyChallenge
            * residualIdentityChallenge
The operator must identify the burden first and the route second.
A report that says only:
“the contract is violated”
is incomplete.
The required question is:
Which burden was violated, and what job does violation of that burden perform?
30.1 Local identity falsifier
A competent constitutive-face violation can bear directly on the proposed identity criterion.
On the DLBTF side, ConstitutiveFaceViolated locates at least one explicitly violated transfer-side constitutive face.
On the HFN side, HistoryFaceViolated locates at least one explicitly violated history-side constitutive face.
Neither predicate is sufficient by itself.
For DLBTF, the surrounding EvaluationAdequate burden must independently support use of the face result.
For HFN, the surrounding HistoryEvaluationAdequate burden must independently support use of the face result.
At a required positive or validly opened holdout, a competent local identity falsifier can therefore contribute to a necessity failure.
At an appropriate negative comparator, the same kind of local failure can instead provide a valid exclusion route.
The machine locates the violated constitutive burden.
The scientific role determines what that violation means for the identity claim.
30.2 Adjudication blocker
Many downstream scientific burdens determine whether a result is competent enough to use without themselves deciding the identity claim.
On the DLBTF side these include, among others:
            * supportAdjudication
            * betaQualification
            * representationNeutrality
            * supportSearchAdequacy
            * powerAdequacy
            * parameterEnlargementRobustness
            * causalNoveltyAdequacy
            * suppressionAdequacy
            * matchedShamAdequacy
            * restorationOrReplacementAdequacy
On the HFN / compound side they include:
            * historyAdjudication
            * historyRepresentationAdequacy
            * historyPowerAdequacy
            * recurrenceExhaustionAdequacy
            * antiReplicationAdequacy
            * externalMimicProvenanceAdjudication
Violation at one of these locations means that the affected result has not earned the scientific authority required for the intended identity inference.
It does not automatically establish the opposite scientific status.
For example:
            * violated causal-novelty adequacy does not establish BOUNDED;
            * violated history power does not establish RECURRENT-THIN;
            * violated provenance adjudication does not establish provenance failure.
Absent some independent earned adverse result, an adjudication blocker leaves the affected global route open.
30.3 Execution-invalid violation
A different class protects the integrity of the confirmatory question itself.
Universal core violations route here.
So do violations of the frozen DLBTF entry surface, including scope, roster, seat identity, role, β definitions, enlargement rules, representation plans, closure accounting, and pre-outcome contract integrity.
The HFN / compound entry surface behaves the same way for its frozen scope, level, family, fidelity, perturbation, quotient, recurrent-template, endogenous-variation, representation, decision, boundary, anti-replication, and pre-outcome rules.
The compound burden:
boundaryLevelCoherence
also routes to execution invalidity when violated.
The scientific meaning is:
the affected execution route no longer represents the frozen confirmatory question.
An invalid route cannot be used as evidence for closure.
It is not automatically evidence against the identity criterion either.
For example, changing the physical level after seeing the B1 result does not establish HFN failure.
It invalidates that boundary route.
30.4 Redundancy challenge
The transfer-side burden:
redundancyDiscrimination
and the compound burden:
compoundRedundancyDiscrimination
perform identity-level challenge jobs.
A violated redundancy burden means the scientific audit has earned the conclusion that a simpler established criterion already performs the relevant complete discrimination job.
That is not a per-seat BOUNDED or RECURRENT-THIN result.
It is a challenge to the independent identity role claimed for the proposed criterion.
When scientifically earned, it routes globally to:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.
If the redundancy audit remains open rather than violated, the result remains unresolved.
30.5 Residual-identity challenge
The compound burden:
residualIdentityNull
asks whether DLBTF ∧ HFN has left out an independent same-level identity burden.
A violated residual-null burden means such a burden has survived the declared tests:
            * it performs a distinct discrimination job;
            * it has its own physical failure condition at the same level;
            * it changes a relevant classification;
            * it cannot be absorbed into DLBTF or HFN.
The machine exposes this through:
ResidualIdentityFound.
Like redundancy, this is not a per-seat transfer or history failure.
It is an identity-level adverse result.
When scientifically earned, it returns:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.
An open residual audit instead remains unresolved.
31. Machine certificates are located statements
The released turbulence bundle contains two related machine-certificate surfaces:

            2. a compound DLBTF + HFN surface.
Several names occur in both namespaces, including:

            * NecessityFail
            * SufficiencyFail
            * Redundant
            * Invalid
            * ContractNotYetAdjudicable
The execution record should therefore qualify the namespace whenever ambiguity is possible.
The machine layer does not collapse all possible conditions into one exclusive outcome enum.
Several located predicates may be reportable in the same scientific record.
A machine certificate is therefore a located formal statement, not a substitute for the final scientific disposition.
Sections 31.1–31.11 describe the compound surface unless stated otherwise.
31.1 Compound IdentitySupported

            * HFN / compound structural admission;

Its formal meaning is:
the supplied machine population satisfies the released compound-support contract.
Its scientific non-meaning is equally important:
turbulence identity has not thereby been empirically proved.
Scientific closure still requires the separate execution-record validity described in Section 32.

holds when at least one:

A scientifically valid compound NecessityFail is enough for:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.

NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.

NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C.

32. Scientific execution-record validity
A formally populated contract can only answer for the science if the machine population faithfully represents the actual frozen-protocol record.
Scientific closure therefore requires more than a successful formal predicate.
The execution record must establish at least the following.
32.1 Exact release binding
The execution identifies:

            * the exact Σ_C declaration;
            * the exact append bundle;
            * DLBTF component version;
            * compound component version;
            * Universal Kernel version;
            * applicable companion guidance.
32.2 Scientific standing and role fidelity
For every seat:
            * the physical case is identifiable;
            * its independent scientific standing is recorded;
            * its scientific role remains the frozen one;
            * its theorem-facing role corresponds to that scientific job.
A machine role cannot create the scientific standing it represents.
32.3 Scope fidelity
Per-seat scope membership or comparator jurisdiction is independently supported.
An outside-scope case is not silently turned into a negative comparator.
A scope question is not rewritten after the result.
32.4 Faithful burden population
Every machine state, status, and evidence witness used in the decisive formal execution must derive from the actual scientific protocol adjudication.
The operator must not populate:
            * discharged;
            * opening;
            * nondegenerate;
            * requiredProvenanceFailed;
            * any other convenient state
merely because that population makes a desired theorem constructible.
The scientific record comes first.
The machine population follows it.
32.5 Holdout integrity
V1/V2 retain:
            * their scientific holdout identity;
            * protection history;
            * opening event;
            * no-tuning record.
Their theorem-facing positiveCore role after opening does not erase that information-order burden.
32.6 Boundary integrity
B1a and B1b retain:
            * distinct identities;
            * distinct frozen levels;
            * level-specific adjudications.
No post-result switch between isolated object and containing regime is used to obtain a favorable result.
32.7 External-mimic provenance fidelity
Where the machine role is externalMimic, the physical-provenance status must correspond to the same scientific object and to a separately competent provenance adjudication.
32.8 Pre-outcome integrity
No decisive:

            * role;
            * physical level;
            * β definition;
            * fidelity rule;
            * comparator standing;
            * decision rule;
            * recurrence rule;
            * support-accounting rule;
            * other frozen confirmatory element
may be changed after observing the outcome in order to rescue the candidate.
32.9 Invalid routes remain invalid
If an execution route is invalidated, that fact is recorded.
The route is not silently repaired inside the same confirmatory record and then treated as if the original test had survived.
Exploratory repair may inform a later independent execution.
It is not the same execution.
33. From machine result to scientific disposition
The final scientific adjudication permits exactly three global outcomes:
            * CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C
            * NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C
            * NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C
There is no fourth global scientific state.
The routing order matters.
33.1 First question: has an adverse result been earned?
Ask first whether the valid scientific record contains an earned result that defeats the proposed identity criterion.
Qualifying routes include:
            * scientifically valid compound NecessityFail;
            * scientifically valid DLBTF necessity failure;
            * scientifically valid compound SufficiencyFail;
            * an earned transfer-side redundancy challenge;
            * an earned compound redundancy challenge;
            * an earned residual-identity challenge.
If one of those results has been earned:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.
This remains true even if an unrelated burden elsewhere is unresolved.
One valid counterexample to necessity does not become less adverse because another flow is underpowered.
One valid sufficiency counterexample does not disappear because a boundary assay elsewhere remains open.
An earned adverse result takes precedence over unrelated uncertainty.
DLBTF-only sufficiency failure is not listed here as an automatic global adverse result because a hard negative that passes DLBTF may still be excluded by HFN in the complete compound test.
33.2 Second question: has complete closure been earned?
If no NOT CLOSED result has been earned, ask whether the complete positive closure burden has survived.
CLOSED requires:
            * a scientifically valid execution record;
            * faithful machine population;
            * every required positive core seat to satisfy both conjuncts;
            * every required opened holdout to satisfy both conjuncts;
            * every hard negative to be competently excluded;
            * every external mimic to be competently excluded through a licensed route;
            * every boundary seat to retain competent level coherence;
            * all required transfer and HFN adequacy burdens discharged;
            * transfer redundancy discrimination discharged;
            * compound redundancy discrimination discharged;
            * residual identity null discharged;
            * compound machine IdentitySupported.
The bridge is therefore:
SCIENTIFICALLY VALID, FAITHFULLY POPULATED EXECUTION RECORD
+
COMPOUND MACHINE IdentitySupported
→
SCIENTIFIC CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.
Neither side of that bridge is dispensable.
33.3 Third question: what remains if neither condition is met?
If no adverse result has been earned and complete closure is not licensed:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C.
This includes cases where a required burden is:
            * unresolved;
            * inadmissible;
            * underpowered;
            * scientifically inadequate;
            * execution-invalid.
Examples include:
            * unresolved β qualification;
            * underpowered CTRR evidence;
            * unresolved competent representation disagreement;
            * inadequate transfer-support discrimination;
            * failed OPENING controls;
            * unresolved HFN history discrimination;
            * unresolved recurrence exhaustion;
            * anti-replication failure;
            * unresolved external-mimic provenance;
            * unresolved boundary coherence;
            * unresolved required holdout;
            * unresolved redundancy audit;
            * unresolved residual-identity audit;
            * frozen-scope, frozen-role, frozen-level, or pre-outcome integrity failure.
The governing principle is:
absence of closure is not evidence against the criterion.
34. Report the narrowest earned result
The scientific report should identify the narrowest burden that actually determines the disposition.
Do not inflate a local result into a broader claim.
Do not compress a blocker into a falsifier.
Do not hide a falsifier behind a generic statement that the package was “inconclusive.”
Examples follow.
If a required positive is competently BOUNDED:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — DLBTF necessity failed at [seat].
If a required positive is competently RECURRENT-THIN:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — HFN necessity failed at [seat].
If a hard negative competently passes the complete conjunction:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — compound sufficiency failed at [seat].
If an external mimic competently passes the compound criterion and satisfies the required provenance:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — external-mimic sufficiency failed at [seat].
If a hard negative passes DLBTF but is excluded by HFN:
DLBTF-only sufficiency pressure occurred at [seat], but the comparator was excluded by HFN at the compound level.
That is not a global NOT CLOSED result.
If transfer power is inadequate and no independent adverse result exists:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C — transfer power remains unresolved at [seat].
If transfer representation neutrality is violated and no independent adverse result exists:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C — competent transfer representations remain unreconciled at [seat].
If anti-replication fails and no independent adverse result exists:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C — the DLBTF OPENING route at [seat] is blocked by anti-replication adequacy.
If a boundary route changes level after the result:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C — the affected boundary execution is invalid because frozen-level coherence was not preserved.
If a simpler established criterion absorbs the complete discrimination job:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — the proposed identity criterion fails the redundancy challenge.
If an independent same-level identity burden survives:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C — the residual-identity null is defeated.
Only when every declared closure burden survives may the report advance to the anchor-owned CLOSED statement.

36. How to use the ledger
For any disputed or unclear result, trace downward rather than guessing upward. Start with the scientific question:

Then locate:
            1. its mapping class;
            2. its machine field or parent predicate;
            3. the adjudicated state supplied to the machine;

43. What a clean Lean run means
A clean execution means that the loaded Kernel, turbulence bundle, and execution-specific declarations are accepted by the Lean environment without unresolved syntax, elaboration, type, or proof errors for the propositions actually constructed.

            * the evidence propositions have supplied proof witnesses;
            * the relevant composite predicates follow from the supplied states;
            * the requested certificates are formally constructible;
            * the released theorem dependencies compose consistently.
That is valuable.
It is the point of the machine layer.
43.1 What a clean Lean run does not mean
A clean execution does not establish the truth of the empirical premises.
It does not establish:
            * that the experiment was performed;
            * that the data are real;
            * that a seat belongs in Σ_C;
            * that a comparator is scientifically admissible;
            * that β was physically preserved;
            * that a representation was competent;
            * that CTRR was measured correctly;
            * that BOUNDED or OPENING is physically true;
            * that NONDEGENERATE or RECURRENT-THIN is physically true;
            * that provenance is physically satisfied or failed;
            * that holdout or boundary integrity was preserved;
            * that the machine was faithfully populated from the scientific record;
            * that the final scientific disposition is CLOSED.
A clean compile is a machine fact.
Scientific closure is a protocol result.

47. Troubleshooting by failure type
When an execution does not produce the expected formal or scientific result, diagnose the failure at the lowest level that actually failed.
Do not begin by asking whether the turbulence criterion is right or wrong.
Ask:
What object failed to perform its declared job?
The main failure classes are different and should remain different.
47.1 Software or environment failure
Examples include:
            * parser error;
            * syntax error;
            * incompatible Lean environment;
            * namespace collision;
            * missing declaration;
            * incorrect load order;
            * elaboration error unrelated to the scientific population;
            * malformed file composition.
These are machine-environment failures.
They have no turbulence interpretation.
Repair the software or environment and rerun the same scientific population.
Do not alter the scientific record to make a software problem disappear.
47.2 Execution-structure or population mismatch
The code may load correctly while the execution-specific object cannot satisfy the expected type or predicate.
Possible causes include:
            * wrong Seat mapping;
            * wrong theorem-facing role;
            * omitted burden;
            * incorrect Disposition;
            * inconsistent support or history status;
            * missing evidence witness;
            * evidence predicate attached to the wrong state;
            * misunderstanding of a released predicate;
            * transcription error between scientific record and machine population.
The first repair target is the crosswalk:
scientific evidence
→ protocol adjudication
→ machine population.
If the crosswalk was entered incorrectly, repair the population.
If the population faithfully represents the record but the released contract cannot represent that record, do not force a local patch.
Record the mismatch as possible package pressure.
47.3 Scientific adjudication blocker
Sometimes the machine faithfully represents the scientific record and the record itself remains inadequate for the intended inference.
Examples include:
            * underpowered CTRR;
            * unresolved representation disagreement;
            * unresolved β qualification;
            * inadequate bounded-interface search;
            * failed OPENING control;
            * inadequate recurrence exhaustion;
            * unresolved history representation;
            * unresolved external-mimic provenance;
            * failed anti-replication adequacy.
This is not a software problem.
It is not a machine defect merely because Lean will not construct the desired pass predicate.
The scientific burden remains open.
Absent another independently earned adverse result:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C.
47.4 Scientific identity falsifier
A different case occurs when the record has competently earned a result that directly defeats necessity or sufficiency.
Examples include:
            * a required positive or validly opened holdout is competently BOUNDED;
            * a required positive or validly opened holdout is competently RECURRENT-THIN;
            * a required positive competently violates a constitutive DLBTF or HFN face under the required adequacy conditions;
            * an admissible hard negative competently passes the complete conjunction;
            * an external mimic competently passes the complete conjunction and satisfies the required provenance.
These are not troubleshooting failures to be repaired away.
They are scientific results.
The appropriate response is to preserve the result and report:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C
with the narrowest earned reason.
47.5 Execution invalidity
An execution may also fail because the confirmatory question was no longer the question that had been frozen.
Examples include:
            * scope changed after outcome inspection;
            * role changed after outcome inspection;
            * β definition changed after outcome inspection;
            * transfer-support definition changed after outcome inspection;
            * closure accounting changed after outcome inspection;
            * recurrent-template class changed after outcome inspection;
            * B1a and B1b physical levels were switched after seeing the result;
            * a protected holdout was tuned after opening.
An invalid execution cannot support CLOSED.
Its result also does not automatically falsify the candidate.
Absent another independently earned adverse result:
NOT YET ADJUDICABLE WITHIN Σ_C — execution invalid at [located burden].
A repaired design belongs to a new execution.
47.6 Redundancy or residual-identity pressure
A simpler established criterion may absorb the complete identity job.
Or an independent same-level identity burden may survive the attempt to close on DLBTF ∧ HFN.
Those are not software failures, ordinary blockers, or per-seat transfer/history statuses.
If scientifically earned:
            * redundancy defeats the independent identity role;
            * surviving residual identity defeats completeness.
The result is:
NOT CLOSED WITHIN Σ_C.
If either audit remains unresolved, the corresponding route remains open instead.
47.7 Possible machine-contract defect
A machine defect is suspected only after the following have been distinguished:
            1. the Lean environment is functioning;
            2. the released files were loaded correctly;
            3. the scientific record is internally coherent;
            4. the protocol adjudication is clear;
            5. the machine population faithfully represents that adjudication;
            6. the apparent contradiction remains in the released encoding.
At that point record:
            * the exact machine object;
            * the exact scientific burden it is supposed to represent;
            * the expected mapping;
            * the actual encoded behavior;
            * the capacity lost or misrepresented.
Do not silently edit the public contract during the same confirmatory execution.
A demonstrated turbulence-specific contract defect pressures the turbulence adapter first.
The Universal Kernel reopens only if the demonstrated defect reaches reusable domain-neutral machine structure.
